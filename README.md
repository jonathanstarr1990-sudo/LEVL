# LEVL

LEVL

What is LEVL?

LEVL is a reputation infrastructure platform for the creator economy.

Its purpose is to create a portable, verifiable reputation layer that exists independently of any individual platform.

Today, creators build audiences across YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, Discord, Kick, X and many other ecosystems. Their reputation, history, performance and credibility are fragmented across those platforms.

LEVL aims to become the system that connects those fragments together.

If LinkedIn became the professional reputation layer for careers, GitHub became the reputation layer for developers, and Stripe became the infrastructure layer for online payments, LEVL aims to become the reputation layer for the creator economy.

---

The Core Problem

The creator economy has grown into a multi-billion dollar industry, but trust infrastructure has not evolved with it.

Creators spend years building audiences and relationships.

Managers spend years developing talent.

Sponsors spend significant budgets on partnerships and campaigns.

Yet there is still no recognised system that allows any participant to quickly answer questions such as:

- Can this creator be trusted?
- Have they delivered successful campaigns before?
- Are their audience metrics credible?
- Who has worked with them previously?
- What type of content performs best?
- What is their professional track record?
- Are they represented?
- Have they been verified?

Most decisions today are still based on:

- Screenshots
- Media kits
- Follower counts
- Manual references
- Platform-specific analytics
- Personal relationships

This creates friction, inefficiency and risk for everyone involved.

LEVL exists to solve that problem.

---

The Vision

The long-term vision is to create a shared reputation network for the creator economy.

A creator should be able to build a reputation once and carry it everywhere.

That reputation should not disappear because:

- They change platform
- They change manager
- They rebrand
- An algorithm changes
- A platform declines

The reputation belongs to the creator.

LEVL becomes the infrastructure that records, verifies and presents it.

Reputation as Infrastructure

The central thesis behind LEVL is:

"Reputation becomes infrastructure."

Most creator platforms focus on:

- Content
- Followers
- Analytics
- Monetisation

LEVL focuses on:

- Trust
- Verification
- Credibility
- Professional identity
- Opportunity

The reputation layer sits above platforms rather than replacing them.

LEVL is not trying to compete with YouTube, Twitch, TikTok or Instagram.

It sits between participants and makes interactions safer, faster and more trustworthy.

---

Product Principles

Every decision should be evaluated against these principles.

Trust First

Everything should reinforce trust.

If a feature increases trust, it is valuable.

If it weakens trust, it should be questioned.

---

Verification Over Claims

Self-reported information is weak.

Verified information is valuable.

The platform should prioritise evidence wherever possible.

---

Portable Reputation

Reputation belongs to the user.

It should not be locked to a single platform.

---

Opportunity Creation

Reputation should create tangible value.

Trust must lead to opportunity.

---

Simplicity

The concept is powerful because it is easy to understand.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

- The recognised reputation layer for creators
