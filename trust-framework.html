How Verification Works

Verified by source,
not by claim.

The word "verified" is doing real work here. A signal is only verified when it can be traced back to an independent source — not to the creator themselves.

—
Platform API connections
Platform data is pulled directly from YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, and other connected APIs. Audience size, engagement metrics, and account longevity are sourced at the API level — not entered by the creator.
—
Counterparty confirmation
Campaign history is confirmed by the sponsor or brand involved — not by the creator alone. A deal is only on record when both parties have acknowledged it.
—
Manager endorsements
Endorsements carry weight only when the endorser holds their own verified record within LEVL. A manager’s endorsement is traceable to their identity and professional standing.
—
Behavioural history and activity records
Consistency signals — publishing frequency, campaign fulfilment rates, deal renewal patterns — are derived from observable behaviour over time, not from stated intentions.
—
Platform review processes
Disputed or edge-case signals go through a structured review before being recorded. Evidence is assessed independently before any signal reaches the public record.

No self-reported activity creates a verified signal on its own. Every verified signal has an independent source that can be traced, examined, and audited.

04 — Integrity

Reputation must be earned.

Every reputation system faces an obvious question: can it be gamed? LEVL is designed around principles that make manipulation structurally difficult — not just against the rules.

01
Signals must be independently verified
Reputation signals are sourced directly from connected platforms, confirmed sponsors, and endorsed managers — not from creators themselves. Self-reported data is clearly distinguished from verified evidence.
02
Relationships must be real
An endorsement is only as valuable as the relationship behind it. LEVL validates that endorsers are genuine platform participants with their own verified records — not anonymous or fabricated accounts.
03
History is permanent and auditable
Once a signal is recorded, it cannot be quietly removed or edited. Reputation history is preserved in full — appeals add verified context and review outcomes to the record, they do not erase it. A creator's full professional history remains intact and auditable.
04
Reputation cannot be purchased
Paying for a subscription gains access to the platform. It does not generate reputation signals. Trust must be earned through verified actions — campaigns delivered, deals renewed, endorsements given by real people.
05
Multiple sources reduce single points of failure
Because reputation is built from many independent signal types, inflating one metric is insufficient. Genuine reputation requires consistent performance across platforms, relationships, and time.
05 — Accountability

Trust requires
accountability.

A reputation system only works if it is fair. Errors happen, relationships change, and circumstances are sometimes complex. LEVL is designed to handle this with transparency and due process — not arbitrary decisions. Signals are not self-editable and cannot be quietly deleted — but disputed records can be reviewed, annotated, and resolved through the process below.

⚖
Signal Reviews
Any signal can be flagged for review. Evidence is examined before action is taken. Speed is secondary to accuracy.
↩
Appeals Process
Creators, managers, and sponsors can contest signals they believe are inaccurate. A successful appeal adds verified context and a review outcome to the record — it does not silently remove it. The original signal and its resolution both remain visible.
◎
Evidence-Based Decisions
Disputes are resolved based on documented evidence — not opinion, seniority, or account size. Both sides have the opportunity to present their case.
🔒
Context Preserved
Where a signal is disputed and context is relevant, that context becomes part of the record — giving future viewers a complete picture, not just a verdict.
Independent Review Process

Disputed signals are reviewed by the LEVL Trust & Integrity team using platform evidence, counterparty submissions, and historical records. Reviews are independent of the parties involved.

Reviews are evidence-based, documented, and auditable. Signals are never silently removed. Outcomes are recorded alongside the original signal history to preserve transparency and context.

The goal is not a perfect system. The goal is a fair and transparent one — where trust is given weight, and where that weight can be examined by anyone looking at the record.
